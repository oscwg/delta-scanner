Pipeline

Each run pulls ten balance-sheet and P&L line items for every SEC filer from the EDGAR XBRL frames API, twelve calendar quarters deep, then keeps NYSE/Nasdaq/Cboe-listed companies with at least $1bn of assets or $500m of trailing revenue. Every company is compared with itself: the latest quarter against the same quarter a year earlier (YoY) and the quarter before (QoQ). Fiscal Q4, which companies report only inside the annual 10-K, is derived as the annual figure minus the first nine months.

Scoring

Each rule scores 0–100 from three parts: size of the % move, materiality (the dollar move as a share of total assets or quarterly revenue) and a robust z-score against the company's own last eight YoY changes. The company score takes the strongest signal in full and adds a damped share of each corroborating one; recent 8-K filings for restatement, bankruptcy, delisting notices, auditor changes, impairments or restructuring raise it further. Confidence is High for directly reported quarterly values, Medium when a fiscal Q4 had to be derived or the company has too little history, and Low when the source looks wrong.

Data gaps

  • Timing. Structured XBRL arrives with the 10-Q/10-K, typically 1–5 weeks after the earnings press release, so the scanner sees results after the market does.
  • Coverage. Foreign filers using 20-F/40-F and IFRS tags are largely absent. Banks and insurers often use different revenue tags, so their P&L rules fire less reliably.
  • Cash flow. Operating cash flow is reported year-to-date in 10-Qs and is not in the quarterly frames, so earnings-quality checks use receivables and inventory instead.
  • Mechanical moves. Acquisitions, spin-offs, stock splits and reclassifications create real-looking deltas. The balance-sheet transformation flag and 8-K Item 2.01 help spot them.
  • Freshness. Each run reads EDGAR's live latest-filings feed (updated within minutes) for 10-Qs, 10-Ks and 8-Ks from the last 48 hours. XBRL figures appear in the data within minutes of a 10-Q being accepted; earnings releases (8-K Item 2.02) carry no XBRL, so they are listed under Just reported until the 10-Q arrives.
  • Statements. Quotes come only from SEC filings: the 10-Q/10-K behind the numbers, the same quarter's earnings release, and any call transcript or prepared remarks the company filed as an exhibit. Third-party transcript sites are not used because they cannot be checked against an official record. A quote is shown only if it is on the flag's topic; it is marked verified only if its figure matches the flagged value.
  • Base effects. A big YoY swing that only reflects a one-off in the year-ago quarter is screened out, so a recovery from last year's impairment does not show up as a new shift.
  • Filer errors. XBRL tagging mistakes happen; moves of roughly 1000× are down-weighted and labelled as possible scaling errors.
  • No prices. Market cap and share-price reaction are not included, to keep the pipeline free of unofficial data sources.
Source: SEC EDGAR XBRL frames API and Submissions API (data.sec.gov). Public domain US government data. Not investment advice.
